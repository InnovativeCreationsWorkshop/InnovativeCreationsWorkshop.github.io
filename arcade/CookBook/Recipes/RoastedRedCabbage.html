ROASTED RED CABBAGE

★

Easy

total time: 40–45 minutes
Crispy-edged red cabbage wedges roasted with garlic, mustard, and balsamic. Finished with cheese and fresh thyme for a savory, tangy side dish.

NOTES

  • Save some of the mustard and olive oil base before brushing the cabbage so you can turn it into the balsamic glaze later.
  • Let the cabbage develop dark, roasted edges before adding the final glaze. Finish with cheese and fresh thyme right before serving.

    • INGREDIENTS

    • 1 red cabbage
    • Mustard
    • Olive oil
    • Garlic, minced
    • Balsamic vinegar
    • Salt and black pepper
    • Cheese, for topping
    • Fresh thyme

    DIRECTIONS

    1. Preheat the oven to 425°F.
    2. Cut the red cabbage into wedges, keeping the wedges intact.
    3. In a small bowl, combine mustard, olive oil, salt, black pepper, and minced garlic to make a seasoning base. Set some of the mixture aside for the glaze.
    4. Brush or paint the cabbage wedges generously with the remaining mustard mixture, coating the cut sides.
    5. Place the cabbage wedges on a baking sheet and roast for about 20 minutes.
    6. While the cabbage roasts, add balsamic vinegar to the reserved mustard mixture and stir to make a dressing or glaze.
    7. After 20 minutes, remove the cabbage from the oven and brush the balsamic mixture over the wedges.
    8. Return the cabbage to the oven and roast for another 15 minutes, or until the edges are deeply roasted and caramelized.
    9. Top with cheese and fresh thyme, then serve warm.
