BEEF BORTHY SOUP

★

Easy

prep time: 15 minutes
cook time: 30 minutes–2 hours
A rich, savory beef soup with red curry paste, aromatic ingredients, and creamy coconut broth, served over rice or noodles.

NOTES

  • The recipe calls for rice, but you can use your noodles of choice, such as soba, udon, or ramen.
  • Beef short ribs are recommended, but any convenient cut of beef works. Cut the beef into chunks for easier cooking.
  • Beef stock can be substituted with bone broth, chicken stock, or vegetable stock. Add water as needed to adjust the broth quantity and flavor.
  • Cooking time depends on the size and cut of beef. Smaller pieces may take around 30 minutes, while short ribs or larger chunks may need up to 2 hours to become tender.
  • Garnish with Thai basil, sliced scallions, and sesame seeds before serving.

INGREDIENTS

  • Beef short ribs or beef of choice, cut into chunks
  • Salt, to taste
  • Red curry paste
  • Shallot, sliced
  • Ginger, sliced or minced
  • Garlic, minced
  • Soy sauce
  • Beef stock, bone broth, chicken stock, or vegetable stock
  • Fish sauce
  • Brown sugar
  • Coconut milk
  • Rice or noodles of choice
  • Thai basil, for garnish
  • Scallions, sliced, for garnish
  • Sesame seeds, for garnish

DIRECTIONS

  1. Season the beef generously with salt.
  2. Heat oil in a pot over medium-high heat. Sear the beef on all sides until nicely browned. Remove the beef and set aside.
  3. In the same pot, add the red curry paste, shallot, ginger, and garlic. Stir and cook until fragrant.
  4. Return the beef to the pot. Add soy sauce, beef stock, fish sauce, and brown sugar. Stir to combine.
  5. Bring the broth to a gentle simmer. Cover partially and cook until the beef is tender, approximately 30 minutes to 2 hours depending on the cut and size. Add more stock or water as needed to keep the beef sufficiently submerged.
  6. While the beef cooks, prepare the rice or noodles according to their package instructions.
  7. Once the beef is tender, remove it from the pot and set aside.
  8. Strain the broth to remove the shallot, ginger, garlic, and other aromatics. Return the strained broth to the pot.
  9. Stir the coconut milk into the broth and heat gently until warmed through. Avoid boiling vigorously.
  10. Place the cooked beef over rice or noodles in serving bowls. Ladle the coconut curry broth over the beef to your liking.
  11. Garnish with Thai basil, sliced scallions, and sesame seeds. Serve hot and enjoy!

USES

  • Rice bowls
  • Soba noodle bowls
  • Udon soup
  • Ramen bowls
  • Comforting beef and coconut curry soup
