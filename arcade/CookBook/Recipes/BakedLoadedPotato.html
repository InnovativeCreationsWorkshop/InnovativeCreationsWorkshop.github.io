BAKED LOADED POTATO

★

Easy

total time: 1 hour 20 minutes
Crispy baked potatoes loaded with savory seasoned beef or pork, buttery potato, tomato sauce, and melted cheese.

NOTES

  • 180°C is 350°F.
  • Ground beef or ground pork both work well for the filling.
  • Add chili flakes to taste for extra heat.

    • INGREDIENTS

    Baked Potatoes

    • Potatoes
    • Olive oil
    • Salt
    • Black pepper
    • Butter

    Meat Filling

    • Onion
    • Garlic
    • Ground beef or ground pork
    • Tomato paste
    • Crushed tomatoes
    • Salt
    • Sugar
    • Chili flakes
    • Smoked paprika
    • Black pepper
    • Cheese

    DIRECTIONS

    1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (180°C).
    2. Brush the potatoes with olive oil and season generously with salt and black pepper.
    3. Bake the potatoes for about 1 hour, or until tender all the way through.
    4. While the potatoes bake, heat a pan over medium heat and sauté the onion and garlic until fragrant.
    5. Add the ground beef or ground pork and cook thoroughly until browned.
    6. Add the tomato paste and crushed tomatoes.
    7. Season with salt, sugar, chili flakes, smoked paprika, and black pepper.
    8. Stir everything together and cook until the filling is well combined and flavorful.
    9. Remove the baked potatoes from the oven and carefully cut them open.
    10. Add a piece of butter inside each potato and gently mash the inside while keeping the potato intact.
    11. Top each potato generously with the beef or pork filling and cheese.
    12. Return the potatoes to the oven and bake until the cheese is melted and bubbly.
    13. Serve hot.
