PASTA AL POMODORO

★

Easy

total time: 20–25 minutes
Simple, bright tomato pasta with blistered cherry tomatoes, garlic, fresh basil, and melty cheese.

NOTES

  • Add blended carrot or a small amount of sugar to balance the acidity of the tomatoes.
  • Reserve some pasta water before draining so you can loosen and emulsify the sauce.

    • INGREDIENTS

    Ingredients

    • Olive oil
    • Cherry tomatoes
    • Salt
    • Black pepper
    • Garlic
    • Fresh basil
    • Spaghetti
    • Fresh Parmesan
    • Shredded mozzarella
    • Pasta water
    • Blended carrot or sugar (optional)

    DIRECTIONS

    1. Heat olive oil in a large pan over medium heat.
    2. Add the cherry tomatoes and season with salt and black pepper.
    3. Cook until the tomatoes begin to blister and soften.
    4. Add garlic and cook until fragrant.
    5. Smash the softened tomatoes with a spoon to release their juices and create a chunky sauce.
    6. Add fresh basil and stir to combine.
    7. Cook the spaghetti and reserve some pasta water before draining.
    8. Add the spaghetti to the tomato sauce along with a spoonful of pasta water.
    9. Add fresh Parmesan and/or shredded mozzarella.
    10. Sauté everything together until the cheese melts and the sauce becomes incorporated with the pasta.
    11. Taste and adjust the seasoning. If the tomatoes are too acidic, add blended carrot or a small amount of sugar to balance the sauce.
    12. Serve hot with additional fresh basil and Parmesan if desired.
