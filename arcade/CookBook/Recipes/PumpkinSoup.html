ROASTED PUMPKIN SOUP

★

Easy

prep time: 15 minutes
Roasted pumpkin and vegetables blended into a smooth, creamy soup with garlic, herbs, and a hint of spice.

NOTES

  • Roast the vegetables until the pumpkin is fork-tender and slightly caramelized for deeper flavor.
  • Strain the blended soup through a sieve for an extra-smooth texture.
  • Adjust the consistency with additional water or bone broth as needed.
  • For extra heat, add chili powder or cayenne pepper to taste.

    • INGREDIENTS

    • 1 pie pumpkin, peeled, seeded, and cubed
    • Tomatoes
    • Bell pepper
    • Carrots
    • Red onion
    • Whole garlic cloves
    • Olive oil
    • Salt and black pepper
    • Bone broth
    • Water
    • Heavy cream
    • Chili powder or cayenne pepper
    • Fresh parsley or other fresh herbs (optional)
    • Pumpkin seeds (optional)
    • Parmesan cheese (optional)

    DIRECTIONS

    1. Preheat the oven to 425°F (220°C).
    2. Cut the pie pumpkin into cubes and remove the seeds and stringy insides. Peel the pumpkin if desired.
    3. Place the pumpkin, tomatoes, bell pepper, carrots, red onion, and whole garlic cloves in an oven-safe pan.
    4. Drizzle with olive oil and season with salt and black pepper. Toss to coat evenly.
    5. Roast for approximately 35 minutes, or until the pumpkin and other vegetables are tender.
    6. Transfer the roasted vegetables to a blender. Add bone broth and water, then blend until smooth. Work in batches if needed, and follow your blender's instructions for hot liquids.
    7. For an extra-smooth soup, pour the puree through a fine-mesh sieve into a pot.
    8. Bring the soup to a gentle simmer. Add more water or bone broth if needed to reach your desired consistency.
    9. Stir in heavy cream and season with additional salt, black pepper, and chili powder or cayenne pepper to taste.
    10. Let simmer gently for a few minutes, stirring occasionally. Avoid boiling after adding the cream.
    11. Serve warm and garnish with fresh herbs, pumpkin seeds, a drizzle of heavy cream, or grated Parmesan cheese.

    GARNISH IDEAS

    • Fresh parsley or other fresh herbs
    • Roasted pumpkin seeds
    • A swirl of heavy cream
    • Freshly grated Parmesan cheese
    • A pinch of cayenne pepper
