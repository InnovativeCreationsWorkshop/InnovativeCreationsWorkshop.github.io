COLD SOBA NOODLES

★

Easy

prep time: 10 minutes | cook time: 5 minutes
Refreshing Japanese-style cold buckwheat noodles served with a savory, lightly sweet dipping sauce and fresh garnishes.

NOTES

  • Follow the cooking time on your soba noodle package, as different brands may require different cooking times.
  • Rinsing the noodles under cold running water after the ice bath helps remove excess starch and keeps them from sticking.
  • Prepare the dipping sauce ahead of time and chill it in the refrigerator for a refreshing meal.
  • Add sliced chili for heat, or adjust the sugar and soy sauce to taste.

    • INGREDIENTS

    • Soba noodles (buckwheat noodles)
    • Water, for boiling
    • Salt, for boiling water
    • Ice, for the ice bath
    • Scallions, thinly sliced
    • Nori, finely sliced
    • Sesame seeds
    • Fresh chili, thinly sliced (optional)
    • Water (for dipping sauce)
    • Soy sauce
    • Mirin
    • Dashi powder
    • Sugar

    DIRECTIONS

    1. Bring a pot of water to a boil and add a little salt.
    2. Add the soba noodles and cook according to the package instructions, usually around 4–5 minutes.
    3. Drain the noodles and immediately transfer them to an ice bath to stop the cooking process.
    4. Rinse the noodles under cold running water to remove excess starch. Drain thoroughly and set aside.
    5. To make the dipping sauce, combine water, soy sauce, mirin, dashi powder, and sugar in a saucepan.
    6. Bring the sauce to a gentle boil, stirring until the sugar and dashi powder dissolve and the ingredients are well combined.
    7. Remove the sauce from the heat and cool it in an ice bath or refrigerate until chilled.
    8. Thinly slice the scallions and chili, if using. Finely slice the nori into delicate strips.
    9. Arrange the chilled soba noodles on a plate or in a shallow serving dish. Garnish with nori and sesame seeds.
    10. Serve the chilled dipping sauce in a separate bowl, topped with scallions and sliced chili to taste.
    11. To eat, pick up a portion of noodles and dip them into the sauce before enjoying.

    GARNISHES

    • Thinly sliced scallions
    • Finely sliced nori
    • Sesame seeds
    • Fresh sliced chili for spice
