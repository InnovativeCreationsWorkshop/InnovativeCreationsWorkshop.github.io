VANILLA BUTTERCREAM

★

Easy

prep time: 15–20 minutes
A smooth, creamy vanilla buttercream made with butter, powdered sugar, heavy cream, and vanilla. Perfect for frosting cakes, cupcakes, and other desserts.

NOTES

  • Use room-temperature butter for the smoothest consistency.
  • Whipping the butter thoroughly helps create a lighter, whiter buttercream.
  • Add powdered sugar gradually to prevent it from spilling out of the bowl.
  • The finished buttercream should be smooth and creamy, with a texture similar to softened cream cheese.
  • Mix on low at the end to reduce air bubbles for a smoother frosting finish.

INGREDIENTS

  • 1 cup unsalted butter (2 sticks), at room temperature
  • 3 cups powdered sugar
  • 3 tbsp heavy cream
  • 2 tbsp vanilla extract
  • Pinch of salt

DIRECTIONS

  1. Add room-temperature butter to a mixing bowl.
  2. Whisk on high speed for at least 10 minutes, until the butter becomes pale, fluffy, and smooth.
  3. Gradually add the powdered sugar, one cup at a time, mixing on high speed until incorporated before adding the next cup.
  4. Add the heavy cream, vanilla extract, and a pinch of salt.
  5. Mix until everything is fully combined and the buttercream is smooth and creamy.
  6. Finish by mixing on low speed to remove excess air bubbles and create a smoother texture.

USES

  • Cake frosting and filling
  • Cupcake frosting
  • Cookie decoration
  • Layer cakes
  • Brownies and dessert bars
  • Piping decorations
