RUSTIC BROCCOLI POTATO SOUP

★

Easy

total time: 35–40 minutes
A cozy, hearty soup packed with tender potatoes, broccoli, garlic, and Parmesan. Rustic, creamy, and perfect for a cold day.

NOTES

  • Use vegetable stock for a vegetarian version, or chicken stock if desired. For bone broth, use equal parts broth and water.
  • Blend the soup completely for a smooth texture, or blend only part of it to keep it rustic and chunky. Add cream for extra richness if desired.

    • INGREDIENTS

    • Olive oil
    • 1 onion, diced
    • Garlic, minced
    • Potatoes, diced
    • Oregano
    • Broccoli, chopped
    • Vegetable or chicken stock
    • Parmesan rind
    • About 1 cup Parmesan cheese, grated
    • Cream (optional)
    • Salt and black pepper

    DIRECTIONS

    1. Heat olive oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add the diced onion and garlic, then cook until fragrant and softened.
    2. Add the diced potatoes and oregano. Cook for a few minutes, stirring occasionally.
    3. Add the broccoli, then pour in the stock. If using bone broth, use equal parts broth and water.
    4. Bring the soup to a boil, then reduce the heat and simmer for about 20 minutes, or until the potatoes and broccoli are tender.
    5. Add the Parmesan rind and let it simmer in the soup for extra flavor.
    6. Remove the Parmesan rind. Blend the soup if you prefer a smoother, creamier texture, or leave it chunky for a rustic soup.
    7. Stir in about 1 cup of grated Parmesan cheese to thicken the soup. Add cream as desired for extra richness.
    8. Season with salt and black pepper. Serve topped with additional Parmesan cheese.
