SAUSAGE FENNEL RIGATONI

★★

Medium

total time: 35–40 minutes
One-pot rigatoni with browned Italian sausage, caramelized fennel and onion, finished creamy with dijon, parmesan, and lemon zest.

NOTES

  • If using bone broth instead of chicken broth, add a bit of extra water.
  • No fresh parsley on hand? Dried parsley or Italian seasoning works fine.
  • Whole milk can be substituted for heavy cream if needed.

    • INGREDIENTS

    Ingredients

    • Italian sausage
    • Fennel bulb, diced
    • Yellow onion, diced
    • Salt, pepper
    • Garlic, minced
    • Crushed fennel seeds
    • Red pepper flakes
    • Tomato paste
    • White wine
    • Chicken broth
    • Rigatoni
    • Heavy cream
    • Dijon mustard
    • Grated parmesan
    • Lemon (zest)
    • Butter
    • Fresh parsley

    DIRECTIONS

    1. Heat oil in a pot. Brown the sausage, then remove and set aside.
    2. In the same pot, saute the onion and fennel until fragrant. Season with salt and pepper.
    3. Add garlic, fennel seed, and red pepper flakes.
    4. Stir in tomato paste and let it caramelize.
    5. Stir in white wine, chicken broth, and rigatoni.
    6. Simmer, stirring occasionally, until the noodles are tender.
    7. Add heavy cream to thicken. Simmer until it thickens further.
    8. Stir in dijon mustard, the browned sausage, parmesan, fresh parsley, and lemon zest. Mix until incorporated.
    9. Garnish with parsley and serve.
