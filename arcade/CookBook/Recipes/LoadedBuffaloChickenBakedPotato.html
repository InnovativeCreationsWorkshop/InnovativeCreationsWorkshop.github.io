LOADED BUFFALO CHICKEN BAKED POTATO

★

Easy

total time: 45–60 minutes
A loaded baked potato topped with hot buffalo chicken, cheese, sour cream, and green onion or chives.

NOTES

  • Follow the Buffalo Chicken Wing Recipe.
  • Breast and boneless thigh is best for this.

    • INGREDIENTS

    • Russet potato
    • Butter
    • Hot buffalo wing
    • Cheese
    • Sour cream
    • Green onion or chives
    • Salt and pepper
    • Buffalo sauce

    DIRECTIONS

    1. Follow the Buffalo Chicken Wing Recipe.
    2. Bake a russet potato until tender. Note you can microwave it from 6–8 min.
    3. Cut down the middle and squeeze the side to puff up the potato.
    4. Add butter until melted.
    5. Then add hot buffalo wing. Note breast and boneless thigh is best for this.
    6. Add more sauce if you desire.
    7. Then top with cheese and microwave for a minute to melt.
    8. Top with sour cream, green onion or chives, salt and pepper for more flavor.
