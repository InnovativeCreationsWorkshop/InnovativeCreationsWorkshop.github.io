MAPO TOFU

★

Easy

prep time: 15 minutes
Soft tofu simmered with mushrooms, garlic, ginger, doubanjiang, Sichuan pepper, and chili for a savory, spicy, and aromatic dish.

NOTES

  • Use soft or silken tofu and handle it gently throughout cooking so the tofu does not break apart.
  • Remove the husks from whole Sichuan peppercorns if grinding them by hand for a smoother texture.
  • Serve over steamed rice and garnish with sliced scallions and white sesame seeds.

    • INGREDIENTS

    • Soft or silken tofu (cubed)
    • Mushrooms (finely diced)
    • Garlic (finely diced)
    • Ginger (finely diced)
    • Doubanjiang
    • Sichuan peppercorns (ground)
    • Gochugaru chili flakes
    • Sugar
    • Cornstarch mixed with water
    • Scallions (sliced)
    • Oil
    • White sesame seeds
    • Steamed rice

    DIRECTIONS

    1. Cut the soft tofu into cubes. Bring a pot of water to a boil and gently boil the tofu for a few minutes to heat it through. Drain carefully.
    2. Finely dice the mushrooms. Heat oil in a pan and sauté the mushrooms until cooked and lightly browned. Remove from the pan and set aside.
    3. Add the finely diced garlic and ginger to the pan and sauté until fragrant.
    4. Add the doubanjiang and stir into the garlic and ginger.
    5. Add the ground Sichuan peppercorns. If grinding whole peppercorns by hand, remove the husks beforehand.
    6. Add gochugaru chili flakes and a small amount of sugar. Stir to combine.
    7. Add water mixed with cornstarch and stir until the sauce begins to thicken.
    8. Add the sliced scallions and simmer the sauce for a few minutes.
    9. Gently add the cooked mushrooms and tofu back into the pan. Carefully fold everything together so the tofu stays intact.
    10. Serve over steamed rice and garnish sesame oil, sliced scallions and white sesame seeds.

    USES

    • Rice bowls
    • Weeknight meals
    • Spicy Sichuan-style dishes
    • Vegetarian meals
