TORTA DE ASADA

★

Easy

total time: 30–40 minutes
A loaded Mexican-style steak torta with savory carne asada, creamy avocado, cotija, fresh toppings, and toasted bread.

NOTES

  • Use skirt steak, chopped carne asada meat, or ground beef depending on what you have.
  • Cover the meat while it cooks so the flavors and juices develop before finishing uncovered.
  • Toast both sides of the bread for a crisp exterior that holds up to the fillings.

    • INGREDIENTS

    Carne Asada

    • Carne asada meat, chopped into cubes
    • Skirt steak or ground beef
    • Fajita seasoning
    • Black pepper
    • Paprika
    • Lime juice
    • Cilantro
    • Onion

    Torta Assembly

    • Bolillo bread or hoagie roll
    • Refried beans
    • Cotija cheese
    • Fresh onion, diced
    • Cilantro
    • Shredded lettuce
    • Avocado
    • Crema

    DIRECTIONS

    1. Add the carne asada meat to a pan and season with fajita seasoning, black pepper, paprika, lime juice, cilantro, and onion.
    2. Cover and cook for 15–20 minutes, allowing the meat to cook in its juices and seasonings.
    3. Remove the cover and continue cooking for another 10 minutes, or until the liquid has cooked off and the meat is fully cooked and flavorful.
    4. Toast the bolillo bread or hoagie roll on both sides until lightly crisp.
    5. Smear refried beans onto the toasted bread.
    6. Add the cooked carne asada.
    7. Top with cotija cheese, diced fresh onion, cilantro, shredded lettuce, avocado, and crema.
    8. Close the torta and serve immediately.
