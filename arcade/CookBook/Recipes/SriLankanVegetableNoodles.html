SRI LANKAN STYLE VEGETABLE NOODLES

```
★

Easy

prep time: 15 minutes
A simple and flavorful vegetable noodle dish with crisp vegetables, scrambled eggs, and optional seasonings and garnishes.

NOTES

  • Keep the vegetables slightly crisp for the best texture.
  • Leek is optional and can be omitted if unavailable.
  • Season with soy sauce or your favorite seasoning to taste.
  • For extra flavor and heat, garnish with shallots, green onions, or crushed chilies.
  • Serve on its own or pair with grilled chicken, beef, fish, or shrimp.

    • INGREDIENTS

    • 2 carrots, julienned
    • 1 cup green beans, diced
    • 2 cups cabbage, finely shredded
    • 1 leek, julienned (optional)
    • 1–2 tablespoons butter
    • 2 eggs
    • 1 packet noodles
    • Soy sauce or favorite seasoning, to taste (optional)
    • Sliced shallots (optional garnish)
    • Chopped green onions (optional garnish)
    • Crushed chilies (optional garnish)

    DIRECTIONS

    1. Julienne the carrots and leek (if using), dice the green beans, and finely shred the cabbage.
    2. Heat the butter in a large pan over medium heat. Add the green beans first and cook until just tender but still crisp.
    3. Add the carrots and cook until slightly softened. Stir in the cabbage, followed by the leek if using, and cook for another 1 to 2 minutes.
    4. Remove the vegetables from the pan and set aside.
    5. In the same pan, scramble the eggs until cooked through. Remove and set aside with the vegetables.
    6. Bring a pot of water to a boil. Cook the noodles for about 3 minutes, or according to the package instructions.
    7. Drain the noodles, rinse under cold water to stop the cooking, then drain well.
    8. Place the noodles, cooked vegetables, and scrambled eggs in a large bowl. Toss everything together until evenly combined.
    9. If desired, season with soy sauce or your favorite seasoning to taste.
    10. Serve on its own or with your favorite protein. Garnish with sliced shallots, green onions, or crushed chilies.

    USES

    • Served as a standalone noodle dish
    • Pair with grilled chicken
    • Pair with grilled beef
    • Pair with grilled fish
    • Pair with shrimp
    • Serve as a side dish
    • Top with fresh shallots or green onions
    ```
