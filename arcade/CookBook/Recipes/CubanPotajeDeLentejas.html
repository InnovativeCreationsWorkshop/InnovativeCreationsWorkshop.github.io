CUBAN POTAJE DE LENTEJAS

★

Easy

total time: 1–1.5 hours
A hearty Cuban lentil stew with smoky chorizo, tender vegetables, rich tomato, and warm spices.

NOTES

  • Use poblano peppers instead of green bell peppers for a deeper, slightly smoky flavor.
  • Skip the pork shank or ham hock if you prefer. Bone broth can be used instead, but add water as well.
  • If using ground chorizo, cook it separately before adding it to the pot.
  • Serve with rice if desired.

    • INGREDIENTS

    Ingredients

    • Olive oil
    • Yellow onion
    • Poblano pepper
    • Garlic
    • Tomato paste
    • Tomato sauce
    • Dry cooking wine
    • Dry lentils
    • Ham hock or pork shank (optional)
    • Spanish chorizo
    • Carrot
    • Potato
    • Cumin
    • Dried oregano
    • Paprika
    • 2 bay leaves
    • Salt
    • Black pepper
    • Chicken stock
    • Water

    DIRECTIONS

    1. Heat olive oil in a large pot over medium heat.
    2. Sauté the onion, poblano pepper, and garlic until fragrant and softened.
    3. Add the tomato paste, bay leaves, cumin, dried oregano, and paprika. Cook down for a few minutes until the tomato paste darkens slightly and the spices become fragrant.
    4. Add your sausage of choice. If using ground chorizo, cook it beforehand and add the browned chorizo to the pot.
    5. Deglaze the pot with some dry cooking wine, scraping up any flavorful browned bits from the bottom.
    6. Add the lentils, tomato sauce, carrot, potato, ham hock or pork shank if using, chicken stock, and water.
    7. Bring to a gentle boil, then reduce the heat and simmer until the lentils are tender and the potatoes are cooked through.
    8. Season with salt and black pepper to taste.
    9. Remove the bay leaves and pork if used. Serve hot, with rice if desired.
