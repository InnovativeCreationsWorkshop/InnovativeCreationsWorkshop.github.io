THAI STYLE SALAD

★

Easy

total time: 10–15 minutes
Very versatile and good pairing for any Asian style dish.

NOTES

  • You can add chicken to it like a rotisserie or pair it with chicken satay.
  • Mango is optional.
  • Optional crispy onion and peanuts.
  • Very versatile and good pairing for any Asian style dish.

    • INGREDIENTS

    • Shredded chicken
    • Green onion or chives
    • Red cabbage
    • Lettuce
    • Mango (optional)
    • Crispy onion (optional)
    • Peanuts (optional)
    • Cilantro
    • Sesame dressing
    • Thai style peanut dressing
    • Spicy Kewpie mayo dressing

    DIRECTIONS

    1. Combine shredded chicken, green onion or chives, red cabbage, lettuce, mango (optional), crispy onion (optional), peanuts (optional), and cilantro.
    2. Use a sesame dressing or a Thai style peanut dressing, or spicy Kewpie mayo dressing for a more coleslaw type salad.
    3. Add chicken to it like a rotisserie or pair it with chicken satay.
