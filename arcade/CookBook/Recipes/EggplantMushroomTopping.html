EGGPLANT AND MUSHROOM TOPPING

★

Easy

prep time: 15 minutes
Pan-fried eggplant and mushrooms cooked in a sweet and savory Japanese-style sauce, served over rice.

NOTES

  • Soaking the eggplant in salted water helps prepare it for cooking and keeps the texture tender.
  • Use a generous amount of oil and high heat to lightly brown the eggplant before adding the mushrooms.
  • Dark soy sauce is mainly used for color, so add only enough to deepen the appearance of the sauce.
  • Serve over rice and garnish with sliced scallions and sesame seeds.

    • INGREDIENTS

    • Eggplant (cut into sticks)
    • Mushrooms (diced)
    • Oil
    • Sake
    • Mirin
    • Soy sauce
    • Sugar
    • Dark soy sauce
    • Steamed rice
    • Sesame seeds
    • Scallions (sliced)
    • Salt

    DIRECTIONS

    1. Dice the mushrooms and cut the eggplant into sticks.
    2. Soak the eggplant in salted water for a short time.
    3. When ready to cook, rinse the eggplant thoroughly and drain well.
    4. Heat a generous amount of oil in a pan over high heat. Fry the eggplant until slightly browned.
    5. Add the diced mushrooms and cook for a few more minutes.
    6. Add the sake, mirin, soy sauce, sugar, and dark soy sauce. Stir to coat the eggplant and mushrooms.
    7. Continue cooking until the sauce has reduced and the eggplant is fully cooked through.
    8. Serve over steamed rice and garnish with sesame seeds and sliced scallions.

    USES

    • Rice bowls
    • Japanese-style meals
    • Vegetarian meals
    • Easy weeknight meals
