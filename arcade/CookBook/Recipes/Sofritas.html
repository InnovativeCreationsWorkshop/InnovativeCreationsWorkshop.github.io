SOFRITAS

★

Easy

total time: 30–40 minutes
Smoky, savory, and spicy tofu sofritas with roasted pepper, chipotle, and warm Mexican-inspired spices.

NOTES

  • Roasting the green pepper first adds extra depth and sweetness.
  • Dry the tofu well before crumbling so it browns instead of steaming.
  • Adjust the chipotle peppers and adobo sauce to control the heat.

    • INGREDIENTS

    Sofritas Marinade

    • Green bell pepper
    • Tomato
    • Onion
    • Water
    • Chipotle peppers in adobo sauce
    • 2 garlic cloves
    • Red wine vinegar (white vinegar can be substituted)
    • Ancho chile powder
    • Ground cumin
    • Dried oregano
    • Salt
    • Black pepper

    Tofu

    • Extra-firm tofu
    • Olive oil

    DIRECTIONS

    1. Roast the green bell pepper until softened and lightly charred.
    2. Sauté the onion, tomato, and garlic until fragrant and soft.
    3. Add the roasted green pepper, sautéed onion and tomato mixture, chipotle peppers with adobo sauce, red wine vinegar, ancho chile powder, ground cumin, dried oregano, salt, and black pepper to a blender.
    4. Add water and blend until the sauce is smooth and well combined.
    5. Dry the tofu thoroughly, then crumble it into small pieces.
    6. Heat olive oil in a pan over medium-high heat and fry the crumbled tofu until browned.
    7. Pour the sofritas sauce over the browned tofu.
    8. Sauté until the tofu is well coated and the sauce cooks into the tofu, becoming flavorful and slightly thickened.
    9. Serve hot.
