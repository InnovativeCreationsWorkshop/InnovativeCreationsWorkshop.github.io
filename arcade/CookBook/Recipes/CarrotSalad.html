KOREAN STYLE CARROT SALAD

★

Easy

total time: 10–15 minutes
A fresh, crunchy carrot salad with a bright, savory dressing.

NOTES

  • Add red cabbage or napa cabbage for added fiber and vegetables.
  • Use sliced jalapeño or a chili of choice.
  • Black sesame looks nice and supports heart health and digestion.

    • INGREDIENTS

    • A few large carrots
    • Red cabbage or napa cabbage (optional)
    • Jalapeño or chili of choice
    • Minced garlic
    • Rice vinegar
    • Soy sauce
    • Mirin
    • Sesame oil
    • Whole lemon or lime
    • Sesame seeds

    DIRECTIONS

    1. Take a few large carrots and shred them or cut them into ribbons.
    2. Add red cabbage or napa cabbage for added fiber and vegetables.
    3. Slice some jalapeño or a chili of choice.
    4. Add the carrots, cabbage, jalapeño or chili, and minced garlic all in a bowl.
    5. For the dressing, combine rice vinegar, soy sauce, mirin, sesame oil, and a whole lemon or lime.
    6. Add the dressing to the salad and mix well.
    7. Top with sesame seeds. Black sesame looks nice and supports heart health and digestion.
