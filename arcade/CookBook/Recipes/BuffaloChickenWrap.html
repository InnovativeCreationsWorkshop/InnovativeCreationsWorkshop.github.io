BUFFALO CHICKEN WRAP

★

Easy

total time: 40–45 minutes
Crispy-edged buffalo chicken, cool ranch, fresh crunch, and melty cheese wrapped up in a warm tortilla.

NOTES

  • Use boneless chicken thighs for a juicier wrap, or chicken breast for a leaner option. Dice the chicken into bite-sized pieces so it cooks evenly.
  • A burrito-size tortilla works great, but a hoagie roll, pita, or lavash can also be used. Warm the bread in the oven for a few minutes until soft and heated through, but don't let it cook or burn.
  • Drain excessive liquid from the chicken after the first 15 minutes so the chicken can roast instead of steam. Make sure the chicken reaches a safe internal temperature of 165°F (74°C) and is no longer pink in the center.
  • For extra buffalo flavor, let the sauce caramelize under the broiler until lightly charred.

    • INGREDIENTS

    • Boneless chicken thigh or chicken breast, diced
    • Salt, Black pepper
    • Garlic powder
    • Buffalo sauce
    • Ranch dressing
    • Burrito-size tortilla
    • Lettuce, chopped
    • Tomatoes, diced
    • White onion, diced
    • Cheese, shredded

    DIRECTIONS

    1. Preheat the oven to 400°F. Dice the boneless chicken thigh or chicken breast into bite-sized pieces. Season with salt, black pepper, and garlic powder.
    2. Spread the chicken evenly in an oven-safe baking dish or on a lined baking sheet. Bake at 400°F for 15 minutes.
    3. Remove the chicken from the oven and mix it around so the pieces cook evenly. If there is excessive liquid in the pan, carefully drain it.
    4. Return the chicken to the oven and bake for another 15 minutes.
    5. Check the chicken for doneness. It should reach an internal temperature of 165°F and be white and fully cooked in the center.
    6. Remove the chicken from the oven and toss thoroughly with buffalo sauce, coating all of the pieces.
    7. Turn the oven to broil. Place the buffalo-coated chicken back under the broiler for about 15 minutes, or until the edges are lightly charred. Watch closely so the sauce doesn't burn.
    8. While the chicken finishes, place a burrito-size tortilla in the hot oven for a few minutes until warmed and flexible. Do not let it cook until crisp or burn. A hoagie roll, pita, or lavash can also be used.
    9. Lay the warm tortilla flat. Add lettuce, diced tomatoes, diced onion, and shredded cheese.
    10. Layer the buffalo chicken over the vegetables, then drizzle with ranch dressing.
    11. Fold in the sides of the tortilla and roll it tightly into a wrap. Serve immediately.
