BUFFALO CHICKEN WRAP

★

Easy

total time: 40–45 minutes
Buffalo chicken wrapped with lettuce, tomatoes, onion, cheese, and ranch.

NOTES

  • Follow the Buffalo Wing Recipe for the wings.

    • INGREDIENTS

    • Boneless chicken thigh or chicken breast, diced up
    • Buffalo sauce
    • Currito size tortilla
    • Lettuce
    • Diced tomatoes
    • Onion
    • Cheese
    • Ranch
    • Hoagie roll, pita, or lavash (optional)

    DIRECTIONS

    1. Follow the Buffalo Wing Recipe for the wings.
    2. For this recipe use boneless chicken thigh or chicken breast diced up.
    3. Put in the oven at 400 for 15 mins then mix, drain if there is excessive liquid, then bake for another 15, check that chick is up to temp or white in the center.
    4. Then throw in the buffalo sauce and broil for 15 min or until lightly charred.
    5. For the wrap, grab a currito size tortilla note you can use a hoagie roll or pita or lavash.
    6. Heat in the hot oven for a few minutes until warmed but not cooked or burned.
    7. Use lettuce, diced tomatoes, onion, cheese, layer buffalo with ranch then wrap.
