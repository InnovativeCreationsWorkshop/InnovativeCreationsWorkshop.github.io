BUTTER CHICKEN CURRY

★

Easy

total time: 45–60 minutes
Creamy butter chicken curry served with rice or roti.

NOTES

  • You can use canned diced tomatoes.
  • You can also use butter chicken powder instead of all the spices if available.

    • INGREDIENTS

    • Boneless chicken breast or thighs, diced
    • Kashmiri chilli powder
    • Turmeric
    • Ginger garlic paste
    • Salt
    • Yogurt
    • Oil
    • Onion
    • Tomatoes
    • Garlic
    • Garam masala
    • A hint of sugar
    • Butter
    • Water
    • Heavy whipping cream
    • Rice or roti

    DIRECTIONS

    1. Boneless chicken breast or thighs, diced, add Kashmiri chilli powder, turmeric, ginger garlic paste, then salt, and yogurt. Let marinade a minimum of 30 min.
    2. Then in a pan use some oil, shallow fry the chicken, then remove from heat.
    3. In the same pot add onion, salt, tomatoes, garlic, salt, garam masala, a hint of sugar, Kashmiri chili powder, and water. Stir well.
    4. Note you can use canned diced tomatoes. You can also use butter chicken powder instead of all the spices if available.
    5. Cook until tomatoes are mushy, then add butter.
    6. Then cool it down and puree/blend the mixture.
    7. In the same pot add butter and then the sauce that was just blended. If it's thick add water to smooth it.
    8. Then add heavy whipping cream, then add chicken and check for salt. Simmer for 5–7 mins.
    9. Finish with a drizzle of heavy cream.
    10. Serve with rice or roti.
