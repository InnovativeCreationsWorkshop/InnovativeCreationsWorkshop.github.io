BAKED POTATO SOUP

★

Easy

total time: 45–60 minutes
A rich, creamy potato soup loaded with bacon, tender vegetables, and classic baked-potato toppings.

NOTES

  • Bone broth can be used instead of chicken broth, but add water as needed.
  • Cook the bacon fully and reserve the rendered fat to sauté the vegetables for extra flavor.
  • Top with shredded cheese, chopped bacon, sour cream, and green onion if desired.

    • INGREDIENTS

    Ingredients

    • Bacon
    • Onion
    • Celery
    • Carrots
    • Garlic, minced
    • Salt
    • Black pepper
    • Parsley
    • Red pepper flakes
    • Chicken broth or bone broth
    • Water (if using bone broth)
    • Russet potatoes, chopped
    • Heavy cream
    • Sour cream
    • Shredded cheese
    • Green onion (optional)

    DIRECTIONS

    1. Add bacon to a large pot and cook until fully cooked and crisp.
    2. Remove the bacon and set aside, leaving the rendered bacon grease in the pot.
    3. Add onion, celery, and carrots to the bacon grease. Stir occasionally and cook for about 5 minutes, or until the vegetables begin to soften.
    4. Add the minced garlic and cook for about 2 minutes until fragrant.
    5. Add salt, black pepper, parsley, and red pepper flakes. Stir well to combine.
    6. Pour in the chicken broth. If using bone broth, add water as needed.
    7. Add the chopped russet potatoes and bring the soup to a simmer.
    8. Cook for 15–20 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the potatoes are tender.
    9. Remove the pot from the heat and stir in the heavy cream and sour cream.
    10. Taste and adjust seasoning as needed.
    11. Top with shredded cheese, chopped bacon, sour cream, and green onion if desired.
    12. Serve hot.
