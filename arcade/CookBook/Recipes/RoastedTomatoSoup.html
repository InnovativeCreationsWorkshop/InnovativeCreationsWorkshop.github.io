ROASTED TOMATO SOUP

★★

Easy

prep time: 15 minutes | cook time: 40 minutes
A rich and creamy roasted tomato soup made with oven-roasted vegetables for deep, fresh flavor.

NOTES

  • Roasting the vegetables develops a sweeter, richer tomato flavor.
  • For extra sweetness, roast a carrot with the vegetables.
  • Add a jalapeño or pepper while roasting for a spicy version.
  • For an extra flavor boost, stir in a pinch of MSG if desired.
  • If the soup is too thick, add more broth or water. If it's still chunky, blend longer and strain for a silky smooth texture.

    • INGREDIENTS

    • 1 bunch tomatoes, halved or quartered
    • ½ whole garlic bulb
    • ½ shallot or ¼–½ sweet onion
    • 1 carrot (optional)
    • 1 jalapeño or pepper (optional)
    • Olive oil
    • Salt & pepper
    • Dried basil
    • Dried oregano
    • Cooking wine
    • Water or broth
    • Fresh basil, chopped
    • Heavy cream
    • Optional: butter, Parmesan cheese, onion powder, garlic powder, MSG

    DIRECTIONS

    1. Preheat oven to 400°F.
    2. Place the tomatoes, garlic, shallot or onion, optional carrot, and optional jalapeño on a baking sheet.
    3. Drizzle generously with olive oil and season with salt, pepper, dried basil, and oregano.
    4. Roast for 30–40 minutes, or until the garlic is soft and the vegetables are lightly caramelized.
    5. Transfer everything to a pot and add a splash of cooking wine. Simmer for a few minutes.
    6. Blend until smooth, then add water or broth until the soup reaches your preferred consistency.
    7. If needed, blend longer and strain through a fine mesh sieve for an ultra-smooth soup.
    8. Stir in fresh chopped basil and heavy cream.
    9. Season to taste and add butter, Parmesan cheese, onion powder, garlic powder, or MSG if desired.

    SERVING IDEAS

    • Grilled cheese sandwich
    • Cheese sticks
    • Crusty bread
    • Garlic bread
    • Parmesan and fresh basil garnish
