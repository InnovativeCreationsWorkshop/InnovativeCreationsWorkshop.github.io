POTATO SAUSAGE SOUP

★

Easy

total time: 40–45 minutes
A hearty, creamy soup loaded with tender potatoes, savory sausage, and classic vegetables. Comfort food in a bowl with a little smoky kick.

NOTES

  • Andouille or kielbasa both work well here. Brown the sausage first to build extra flavor, then add it back near the end.
  • If using bone broth, use equal parts broth and water. Finish with fresh parsley for a bright contrast to the rich, creamy soup.

    • INGREDIENTS

    • Butter
    • Andouille or kielbasa sausage
    • Onion, diced
    • Celery, diced
    • Carrots, diced
    • Potatoes, diced
    • Garlic, minced
    • Paprika
    • Dried thyme
    • Flour
    • Salt and black pepper
    • Stock or broth
    • Heavy cream
    • Fresh parsley

    DIRECTIONS

    1. Dice the onion, celery, carrots, and potatoes. Set the potatoes aside.
    2. Melt butter in a large pot over medium heat. Add the sliced or chopped sausage and cook until browned. Remove the sausage from the pot and set aside.
    3. Add more butter to the pot if needed. Add the onion, celery, and carrots, then season with minced garlic, paprika, dried thyme, flour, salt, and black pepper.
    4. Cook the vegetables and seasonings until fragrant and the flour is incorporated.
    5. Add the diced potatoes and pour in the stock. If using bone broth, use equal parts bone broth and water.
    6. Bring the soup to a simmer and cook until the potatoes and vegetables are tender.
    7. Stir in the heavy cream and return the cooked sausage to the pot. Simmer for a few more minutes to warm everything through.
    8. Season with additional salt and black pepper to taste.
    9. Serve topped with fresh parsley.
